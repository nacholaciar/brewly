# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or sexualised conduct in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical.

Until a private project contact is published, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting only for security matters. For conduct reports, contact the repository owner through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters.
